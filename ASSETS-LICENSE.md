## Generation note

The source sheet was generated for ChickGraph from this art direction:

> A clean 2x2 sprite sheet on a flat chroma background containing four original,
> round, front-facing baby chicken game characters in cyan, coral red, lime green,
> and yellow. Keep the same silhouette and proportions across variants: spherical
> body/head, tiny wings, orange beak and feet, glossy black eyes, polished 3D emoji
> rendering, no text, no accessories, no copied trademarked details.

The sheet was chroma-keyed, cropped, and normalized into transparent 512x512 PNG
sprites. The generated source sheet and intermediate files are intentionally not
committed.
